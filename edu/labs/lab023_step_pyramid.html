✨ Intuitive Concept: The Monument of Pairs

In architecture, pyramids stand for millennia because their broad base supports each ascending tier.

In this Goldbach Step Pyramid, every tier represents an even integer, and every carved stone block is a valid prime pair $(p, q)$.

Notice that there are NO GAPS! Every single tier has at least one sturdy stone block supporting the tiers above it. If an even number ever lacked a prime pair, the pyramid would have a gaping structural hole and collapse!

🎯 Try This! (Hands-On Challenges)

Challenge 1 Count the blocks on the top 4 layers ($N = 4, 6, 8, 12$). How many blocks are on each?
Each of these layers has exactly 1 block! They are the Solo Four from Lab 001 ($2+2=4$, $3+3=6$, $3+5=8$, $5+7=12$).
Challenge 2 Which tier is the first to have 3 separate stone blocks?
Tier N = 24 (3 blocks: $5+19, 7+17, 11+13$) and Tier N = 30 (3 blocks: $7+23, 11+19, 13+17$). Multiples of 6 provide the strongest structural pillars!
Challenge 3 Why does the pyramid get wider and wider as you build downwards?
Because the prime counting function $\pi(N)$ grows, giving larger even numbers more prime candidates and on average more Goldbach pairs ($\sim N / \ln^2 N$)!

📐 Mathematical Rigor & Formal Definition

The total volume of the Goldbach step pyramid up to height $M \in 2\mathbb{N}$ is the cumulative summatory function:

V(M) = \sum_{k=2}^{M/2} g(2k)

By the Hardy-Littlewood asymptotic relation, the mean value satisfies:

V(M) \sim \frac{M^2}{4 \ln^2 M}

guaranteeing quadratic growth of the foundation support blocks with zero interior voids.
