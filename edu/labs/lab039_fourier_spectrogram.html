✨ Intuitive Concept: The Sound of the Primes

If you play the numbers of the Goldbach Comet like notes on a synthesizer keyboard, what chords would you hear?

Because multiples of 6 get a $2.0\times$ boost every 3 even steps ($6, 12, 18, 24...$), the signal has a roaring period 3 fundamental bass note!

Superimposed on top of that is a softer period 5 overtone (from multiples of 30) and a subtle period 7 overtone (from multiples of 42). The comet is literally a Fourier musical chord constructed by prime numbers!

📐 Mathematical Rigor & Formal Definition

The discrete Fourier transform of the normalized signal $x[n] = g(2n) / (2n / \ln^2(2n))$ is:

X[k] = \sum_{n=1}^N x[n] e^{-2\pi i k n / N}

The power spectral density $|X[k]|^2$ exhibits discrete delta spikes at frequencies $\omega_p = 1/p$ for odd primes $p$, proving that the singular series $\mathfrak{S}(N)$ is an almost periodic function with Bohr spectrum $\mathbb{Q} / \mathbb{Z}$.
