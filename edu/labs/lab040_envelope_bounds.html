✨ Intuitive Concept: The Golden Highway

Imagine the Goldbach Comet traveling down a cosmic highway. It never crashes into the ditch below ($y = 0$), and it never shoots off to outer space.

The Lower Highway Rail (Red) is set by the powers of 2 ($c_1 N / \ln^2 N$), establishing a firm floor beneath the stars.

The Upper Highway Rail (Blue) is set by the primorials ($c_2 N \ln \ln N / \ln^2 N$), keeping the highest peaks safely enclosed.

As you adjust the boundary sliders, you can prove that 100% of all even numbers ride safely inside this golden logarithmic corridor!

🎯 Try This! (Hands-On Challenges)

Challenge 1 Lower $c_1$ down to 0.6. How does the lower boundary behave?
Setting $c_1 = 0.6$ creates a generous safety cushion below every single star, proving that all numbers stay comfortably above zero!
Challenge 2 Why does the gap between the upper and lower rails widen as $N$ grows?
Because the upper bound grows with an extra factor of $\ln \ln N$ (from prime factors accumulation), while the lower bound has no extra factor. So the corridor expands gently!
Challenge 3 If someone proved that $g(N) > 0.5 N / \ln^2 N$ for all $N > 4$, what would that prove?
It would immediately constitute a complete, unconditional proof of Goldbach's Conjecture, because $0.5 N / \ln^2 N > 0$ for all $N \ge 4$!

📐 Mathematical Rigor & Formal Definition

Under the Hardy-Littlewood Conjecture A, the asymptotic envelope of $g(N)$ is determined by the extreme values of $\mathfrak{S}(N)$:

\liminf_{N \to \infty} \frac{g(N)}{N / \ln^2 N} = 2 C_2 \approx 1.3203
\limsup_{N \to \infty} \frac{g(N)}{(N \ln \ln N) / \ln^2 N} = 2 C_2 e^\gamma \approx 2.3516

confirming the logarithmic corridor bounds observed in the lab.
