✨ The Spell of Complementary Forces

In classical mythology and game mechanics, bosses possess defensive shields that can only be shattered by harmonizing twin elemental forces.

In the Goldbach arena, each shield is an even number $N$. The secret key to piercing the shield is selecting two prime frequencies $(p, q)$ whose constructive wave interference lands squarely on $N$: $p + q = N$.

Because the dragon's armor numbers grow larger with each phase ($36 \to 54 \to 70 \to 84$), players naturally experience the growing density of Goldbach pairs—there are always multiple ways to defeat each phase!

📐 Energy Conservation & Partition Sieve

In physical models, energy conservation states $E_1 + E_2 = E_{\text{target}}$. In the discrete prime ring, the partition indicator function is defined as:

I_N(p, q) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } p, q \in \mathbb{P} \text{ and } p + q = N \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}

Since the Goldbach representation count is $g(N) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{p < N} I_N(p, N-p) \ge 1$ for all even $N \ge 4$, a valid attacking strike is mathematically guaranteed to exist for every shield phase.
