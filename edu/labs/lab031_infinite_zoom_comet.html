✨ Intuitive Concept: A Galaxy of Numbers

When you plot the number of Goldbach pairs $g(N)$ on the vertical axis against $N$ on the horizontal axis, you don't get a single line: you get a shimmering cosmic comet!

The comet splits into distinct layered bands:

  • The Bright Upper Tail (Cyan): Multiples of 6 ($6k$) shoot upward because they escape divisibility by 3.
  • The Desert Floor (Green & Purple): Numbers of the form $6k+2$ and $6k+4$ form the lower boundaries.
  • Super-Peaks: Multiples of 30 ($2 \times 3 \times 5$) and 210 ($2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7$) explode like fireworks above the main body!

📐 Mathematical Rigor & Formal Definition

The Goldbach counting function $g(N)$ exhibits asymptotic multi-valued distribution governed by:

g(N) = \mathfrak{S}(N) \frac{N}{2 \ln^2 N} (1 + o(1))

where the singular series $\mathfrak{S}(N)$ takes discrete values determined by the radical $\text{rad}(N)$. The multi-band striations correspond to level sets of the arithmetic function $\mathfrak{S}(N)$.
