📐 Epistemology of Mathematical Conjectures

A conjecture $C$ holds the epistemic status of unproven truth across an infinite domain:

\forall N \in 2\mathbb{N}_{\ge 4}: \quad g(N) \ge 1

Empirical verification up to $N = 4 \times 10^{18}$ offers zero guarantee of universal truth (as exemplified by the Skewes number or Pólya's conjecture, which fail only at astronomical scales). Only deductive proof across major and minor arcs can confer theorem status.
