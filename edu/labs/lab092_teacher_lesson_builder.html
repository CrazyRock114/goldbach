✨ Classroom-Ready Pedagogy

This tool bridges interactive web simulations with classroom assignments.

Teachers can create scaffolded discovery worksheets that guide students from concrete arithmetic verification toward abstract algebraic reasoning:

  • Elementary Level: Students practice parity decomposition (Odd + Odd = Even) and small prime hunting ($N \le 30$).
  • Middle School Level: Students explore the $2\times$ multiple-of-6 bonus and find twin prime midpoints.
  • High School Level: Students tackle ternary partitions and Hardy-Littlewood estimations.
