✨ Intuitive Concept: The Sound of Interference

In physics, when two water ripples or light waves cross, they add together. If two crests meet at the same location, they double in height (constructive interference).

Imagine emitting wave ripples from source 0 whose peaks sit on prime numbers ($p$). From source $N$, another set of ripples travels backwards ($N - q$).

Wherever the forward ripples and backward ripples align simultaneously, you get a double-height Goldbach crest! This directly mirrors the Hardy-Littlewood Circle Method in modern number theory, where primes are treated as Fourier wave harmonics!

📐 Mathematical Rigor & Formal Definition

Let $f(\alpha) = \sum_{p \le N} e^{2\pi i p \alpha}$ be the exponential prime generating sum. The Fourier convolution gives:

\int_0^1 f(\alpha)^2 e^{-2\pi i N \alpha} d\alpha = \sum_{p_1 + p_2 = N} 1 = g(N)

The wave superposition displayed directly computes the discrete spatial representation of this Fourier inversion.
