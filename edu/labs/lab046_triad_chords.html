✨ Intuitive Concept: The Music of the Spheres

Pythagoras discovered 2,500 years ago that musical harmony comes from simple mathematical ratios: a vibrating string halved produces an octave ($2:1$), three-halves produces a perfect fifth ($3:2$), and five-fourths produces a major third ($5:4$).

In ternary Goldbach, every odd number decomposes into three notes $(p_1, p_2, p_3)$. These three primes vibrate together to form a rich acoustic triad.

📐 Mathematical Rigor & Formal Definition

The frequency map $\nu: \mathbb{P} \to \mathbb{R}^+$ is an injective homomorphism into the logarithmic pitch space $\mathbb{R} / 12\mathbb{Z}$:

\nu(p) = f_0 \cdot 2^{(\text{rank}(p) \bmod 12) / 12}

The chord soundwave $S(t) = \sum_{j=1}^3 A_j \sin(2\pi \nu(p_j) t)$ illustrates the multi-frequency interference modeling the major arc Fourier components in the circle method.
